# 10-Day Dubai Itinerary: Comprehensive Travel Guide

## Overview
This 10-day Dubai itinerary offers a perfect blend of modern marvels, cultural experiences, desert adventures, and relaxation. Designed for first-time visitors, it covers iconic landmarks, authentic Emirati culture, world-class shopping, and a memorable day trip to Abu Dhabi.

**Best Time to Visit:** November to March (pleasant weather, 20-30°C)
**Currency:** UAE Dirham (AED) - 1 USD ≈ 3.67 AED
**Language:** Arabic (official), English widely spoken
**Time Zone:** GST (UTC+4)

---

## Day-by-Day Schedule

### **Day 1: Arrival & Downtown Dubai Exploration**
**Theme:** Settling in and first impressions of modern Dubai

**Morning:**
- Arrive at Dubai International Airport (DXB)
- Transfer to hotel (Deira/Downtown area recommended for first-timers)
- Check-in and freshen up

**Afternoon:**
- Light lunch at hotel or nearby café (try Al Mallah for authentic shawarma, AED 12-15)
- Rest and adjust to timezone

**Evening:**
- Visit **Dubai Mall** (world's largest shopping mall)
- Watch the free **Dubai Fountain show** (shows every 30 minutes from 6 PM to 11 PM)
- Dinner at Dubai Mall Food Court (variety of options, AED 25-40 per person)

**Food Spots:**
- Breakfast: Hotel or local bakery (AED 15-25)
- Lunch: Al Mallah or similar (AED 12-15)
- Dinner: Dubai Mall Food Court (AED 25-40)
- Snacks: Dates and Arabic coffee (AED 5-10)

**Transport:** Taxi from airport to hotel (AED 50-70) or Metro (Red Line, AED 5-7)
**Daily Transport Cost:** AED 10-15
**Estimated Daily Budget:** AED 200-250 (excluding accommodation)

---

### **Day 2: Iconic Landmarks - Burj Khalifa & Dubai Mall**
**Theme:** Modern Dubai's architectural wonders

**Morning:**
- Early breakfast at hotel (AED 20-30)
- Visit **Burj Khalifa** - "At the Top" experience (Levels 124 & 125)
  - *Book tickets in advance* (AED 149 for standard, AED 378 for sunset)
  - Best time: 8:30 AM to avoid crowds
- Explore Dubai Aquarium & Underwater Zoo (inside Dubai Mall, AED 120)

**Afternoon:**
- Lunch at Souk Al Bahar (traditional market adjacent to Dubai Mall, AED 30-50)
- Stroll through Souk Al Bahar for souvenirs and traditional crafts
- Visit Dubai Dino exhibit (free)

**Evening:**
- Relax at Burj Khalifa Lake promenade
- Dinner at Armani Hotel or similar (AED 80-120) or more budget-friendly options in Dubai Mall
- Optional: Evening fountain show from different angle

**Food Spots:**
- Breakfast: Hotel (AED 20-30)
- Lunch: Souk Al Bahar (AED 30-50)
- Dinner: Dubai Mall restaurants (AED 40-80)
- Snacks: Fresh juice from juice bars (AED 10-15)

**Transport:** Walking/Dubai Mall Metro link (AED 5-10 for day)
**Daily Transport Cost:** AED 10
**Estimated Daily Budget:** AED 350-450 (including Burj Khalifa tickets)

---

### **Day 3: Old Dubai - Cultural Heritage**
**Theme:** Authentic Emirati culture and history

**Morning:**
- Breakfast at local café (AED 15-25)
- Visit **Al Fahidi Historic District** (Al Bastakiya)
  - Explore wind-tower architecture, art galleries, and cultural centers
  - Free entry, open 9 AM - 5 PM
- Visit **Dubai Museum** (Al Fahidi Fort, AED 3)
  - Open 8:30 AM - 8:30 PM (Sat-Thu), 2:30 PM - 8:30 PM (Fri)

**Afternoon:**
- Traditional **abra ride** across Dubai Creek (AED 1 per person)
- Explore **Gold Souk** (free to browse, gold prices vary)
- Explore **Spice Souk** (free to browse, great for souvenirs)
- Lunch at Local House or similar authentic Emirati restaurant (AED 25-35)

**Evening:**
- Visit **Sheikh Saeed Al Maktoum House** (AED 3)
- Dinner at Al Fanar Restaurant (authentic Emirati cuisine, AED 40-60)
- Optional: Evening abra ride to see creek lights

**Food Spots:**
- Breakfast: Local café (AED 15-25)
- Lunch: Local House or similar (AED 25-35)
- Dinner: Al Fanar Restaurant (AED 40-60)
- Snacks: Arabic coffee and dates (AED 5-10)

**Transport:** Metro to Al Fahidi (Green Line, AED 5-7) + Abra (AED 2 round trip)
**Daily Transport Cost:** AED 12-14
**Estimated Daily Budget:** AED 150-200

### **Day 4: Beach & Marina Leisure**
**Theme:** Modern coastal lifestyle and relaxation

**Morning:**
- Breakfast at hotel (AED 20-30)
- Morning at **JBR Beach** (Jumeirah Beach Residence)
  - Free public beach, water sports available (jet skiing AED 150-200 for 15 mins)
- Walk along **The Walk at JBR** (outdoor shopping and dining promenade)

**Afternoon:**
- Lunch at The Walk (variety of international cuisines, AED 40-70)

- Optional: Visit Ain Dubai (world's largest observation wheel, AED 100-150)

**Evening:**
- Sunset at **Dubai Marina** promenade
- Dinner at Pier 7 or similar marina restaurant (AED 60-100)
- Optional: Marina yacht cruise (AED 150-250 for 2 hours)

**Food Spots:**
- Breakfast: Hotel (AED 20-30)
- Lunch: The Walk restaurants (AED 40-70)
- Dinner: Marina restaurants (AED 60-100)
- Snacks: Ice cream or fresh coconut water (AED 10-15)

**Transport:** Metro to DMCC (Red Line, AED 5-7) + tram/walking
**Daily Transport Cost:** AED 10-15
**Estimated Daily Budget:** AED 250-350 (without water sports/yacht)

### **Day 5: Desert Safari Adventure**
**Theme:** Authentic Arabian desert experience

**Full Day Desert Safari** (Book with licensed operator):
- **Pickup:** 2:30 PM - 3:30 PM from hotel
- **Activities:**
  - Dune bashing in 4x4 vehicle (45-60 minutes)
  - Camel riding (15-20 minutes)
  - Sandboarding (optional)
  - Visit to Bedouin-style camp
  - Traditional Arabic coffee and dates welcome
  - Henna painting
  - Shisha smoking (optional)
  - BBQ dinner with unlimited soft drinks
  - Live entertainment: Belly dancing, Tanoura show, Fire show
- **Return:** 9:00 PM - 10:00 PM to hotel

**Food Included:** BBQ dinner with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options
**Cost:** AED 150-200 per person (shared transfer), AED 250-350 (private vehicle)

**Food Spots:**
- Breakfast: Hotel (AED 20-30)
- Lunch: Light lunch before safari (AED 20-30)
- Dinner: Included in safari package
- Snacks: Provided at camp (Arabic coffee, dates, snacks)

**Transport:** Included in safari package (pickup/drop-off)
**Daily Transport Cost:** AED 0 (included)
**Estimated Daily Budget:** AED 200-250 (breakfast + lunch + safari)

**Evening:**

- Optional: Visit La Mer beach area for evening relaxation

**Food Spots:**
- Breakfast: Hotel (AED 20-30)
- Lunch: Emirates Towers area (AED 30-50)
- Dinner: Casual dining (AED 40-70)
- Snacks: Arabic coffee and dates (AED 5-10)

**Transport:** Metro/Taxi between locations (AED 15-20)
**Daily Transport Cost:** AED 15-20
**Estimated Daily Budget:** AED 250-350

### **Day 7: Day Trip to Abu Dhabi**
**Theme:** Capital city exploration and cultural landmarks

**Full Day Abu Dhabi Trip** (~1.5 hours each way):
- **Departure:** 7:00 AM from Dubai
- **Return:** 9:00 PM to Dubai

**Attractions in Abu Dhabi:**
1. **Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque**
   - Free entry, modest dress required (abaya and sheila provided for women)
   - Open 9 AM - 10 PM (closed Fridays 12-2 PM for tourists)
   - Allow 1.5-2 hours

2. **Louvre Abu Dhabi**
   - *Book tickets in advance* (AED 63)
   - Open 10 AM - 6:30 PM (closed Mondays)
   - Allow 2-3 hours

3. **Qasr Al Watan (Presidential Palace)**
   - AED 60 (includes gardens and palace)
   - Open 10 AM - 8 PM
   - Allow 1.5-2 hours

4. **Optional:** Emirates Palace visit (exterior viewing, free) or Ferrari World (AED 295+)

**Evening Stop (Seasonal):**
- **Global Village** (October-April only)
  - Entry fee: AED 15
  - Open 4 PM - Midnight (weekdays), 4 PM - 1 AM (weekends)
  - Cultural pavilions, shopping, entertainment, international food

**Food Spots:**
- Breakfast: Pack from hotel or grab en route (AED 15-25)
- Lunch: Abu Dhabi food court or marina (AED 30-50)
- Dinner: Global Village or return to Dubai (AED 30-60)
- Snacks: Dates, nuts, water (AED 10-15)

**Transport:** 
- Option 1: Book organized tour (AED 200-250 includes transport and guide)
- Option 2: Rent car (AED 150-200/day + fuel) or hire private driver (AED 400-500/day)
- Option 3: Public bus (E100 from Ibn Battuta Metro, AED 25 each way) + local transport

**Daily Transport Cost:** AED 50-100 (depending on option)
**Estimated Daily Budget:** AED 300-400 (including attractions and transport)

## Practical Tips & Essential Information

### **Visa Requirements**
- **GCC Citizens:** Visa-free entry
- **Many Nationalities:** Visa on arrival (30-90 days, check eligibility)
- **Others:** Pre-arranged visa required (apply through airline, hotel, or UAE embassy)
- **Passport Validity:** Minimum 6 months from entry date
- **Check:** [UAE Government Portal](https://www.u.ae/en) for latest requirements

### **Weather & Best Time to Visit**
- **Peak Season:** November to March (20-30°C, pleasant)
- **Shoulder Season:** April & October (25-35°C, warming up)
- **Summer:** May to September (35-45°C+, very hot, humid)
- **Ramadan:** Dates vary yearly, check calendar (modified hours for attractions)

### **Dress Code & Cultural Etiquette**
- **Public Places:** Modest clothing recommended (shoulders and knees covered)
- **Beaches/Pools:** Swimwear acceptable, but cover up when leaving beach area
- **Religious Sites:** Modest dress required (women: headscarf, long clothing; men: long pants)
- **Malls/Restaurants:** Casual to smart casual acceptable
- **Public Displays of Affection:** Avoid (can result in fines)
- **Photography:** Ask permission before photographing people, especially women

### **Currency & Payments**
- **Currency:** UAE Dirham (AED)
- **Exchange Rate:** 1 USD ≈ 3.67 AED (fixed)
- **ATMs:** Widely available, accept international cards
- **Credit Cards:** Visa/Mastercard widely accepted, American Express less common
- **Tipping:** 10% customary in restaurants if service charge not included
- **Bargaining:** Expected in souks, not in malls or fixed-price establishments

### **Transportation Guide**
- **Dubai Metro:** 
  - Red Line (Rashidiya → UAE Exchange)
  - Green Line (Etisalat → Creek)
  - Operating Hours: 5 AM - 12 AM (Sat-Wed), 5 AM - 1 AM (Thu), 10 AM - 12 AM (Fri)
  - NOL Card: Required (AED 25 for card + minimum balance)
  - Fares: Based on zones (AED 3-8.50 per trip)
- **Buses:** Extensive network, NOL card accepted
- **Tram:** Dubai Marina and Al Sufouh areas
- **Taxis:** 
  - Cream-colored with roof sign
  - Minimum fare: AED 12
  - Per km: AED 1.96-2.20
  - Apps: Careem, Uber, Dubai Taxi
- **Rideshare:** Careem and Uber widely used
- **Water Transport:** Abras (AED 1), water taxi, ferries

### **Communication & Connectivity**
- **SIM Cards:** 
  - Etisalat and du are main providers
  - Tourist SIMs available at airport (AED 50-100 for data package)
  - Required: Passport for registration
- **Wi-Fi:** Free in most malls, hotels, cafes, and public areas
- **Country Code:** +971
- **Emergency:** 999 (police, ambulance, fire)

### **Health & Safety**
- **Tap Water:** Safe to drink, but bottled water preferred for taste
- **Healthcare:** Excellent private and public facilities
- **Insurance:** Travel insurance highly recommended
- **Pharmacies:** Widely available (look for green cross sign)
- **Safety:** Dubai is one of the safest cities globally (low crime rate)
- **Laws:** Strict drug laws, zero tolerance; respect local customs and laws

### **Shopping Tips**
- **Tax-Free Shopping:** Available for tourists (minimum spend AED 250)
- **Refund Process:** At airport before departure
- **Best Souks:** 
  - Gold Souk: Jewelry
  - Spice Souk: Saffron, spices, nuts, dried fruits
  - Textile Souk: Fabrics, pashminas, traditional clothing
  - Perfume Souk: Arabian oud, bakhoor, perfumes
- **Shopping Festles:** Dubai Shopping Festival (Dec-Jan), Dubai Summer Surprises (Jun-Aug)

### **Cultural Experiences to Prioritize**
1. **Al Fahidi Historic District** - Authentic old Dubai
2. **Sheikh Mohammed Centre for Cultural Understanding** - Cultural meals and mosque visits
3. **Traditional Abra Ride** - Dubai Creek crossing
4. **Camel Market** (Al Ain Road area) - Early morning visit
5. **Heritage Village** (in Abu Dhabi or Dubai Creek area)
6. **Arabic Coffee Ceremony** - Available at many cultural centers
7. **Falconry Demonstrations** - Desert safaris or dedicated centers

### **Day Trip Logistics to Abu Dhabi**
- **Distance:** ~140 km (87 miles) from Dubai
- **Travel Time:** 1.5-2 hours each way (depending on traffic)
- **Best Route:** Sheikh Zayed Road (E11)
- **Border Check:** None between emirates
- **Fuel Stations:** Plentiful along route
- **Rest Stops:** Available with food, fuel, and facilities

### **Seasonal Considerations**
- **November-March:** Peak tourist season, book accommodations early
- **December:** Dubai Shopping Festival, Christmas/New Year celebrations
- **January-February:** Pleasant weather, Dubai Marathon (January)
- **March:** Weather warming up, still good for outdoor activities
- **April-October:** Hotter, focus on indoor activities, water parks, evening outings
- **Ramadan:** Modified hours, Iftar experiences available after sunset
- **Summer:** Indoor attractions, shopping malls, hotel pools, early morning/late evening outdoor activities

### **Emergency Information**
- **Police:** 999
- **Ambulance:** 999
- **Fire:** 999
- **Tourist Police:** Available in major tourist areas
- **Your Country's Embassy:** Keep contact information handy
- **Hotel Address:** Keep written copy in Arabic and English for taxi drivers

### **Packing Suggestions**
- **Clothing:** Light, breathable fabrics; modest clothing for public areas
- **Footwear:** Comfortable walking shoes, sandals for beach
- **Sun Protection:** High SPF sunscreen, sunglasses, hat
- **Electronics:** Universal adapter (UK-type plugs), camera/phone charger
- **Documents:** Passport, visa, travel insurance, hotel confirmations
- **Extras:** Reusable water bottle, small backpack for day trips, medications

### **Useful Apps**
- **RTA Dubai:** Public transport planner and NOL card top-up
- **Careem/Uber:** Ride-hailing
- **Entertainer:** Buy-one-get-one-free offers for dining/attractions
- **The Entertainer:** Similar discount app
- **Zomato/Deliveroo:** Food delivery
- **Visit Dubai:** Official tourism app
- **Dubai Now:** Government services
- **Google Maps:** Navigation (generally accurate)

### **Cultural Sensitivity Reminders**
- **During Ramadan:** No eating, drinking, or smoking in public during daylight hours
- **Photography:** Avoid photographing government buildings, military sites, or people without consent
- **Language:** Arabic phrases appreciated: "Shukran" (thank you), "Marhaba" (hello), "Afwan" (you're welcome)
- **Friday:** Holy day, some attractions may have modified hours
- **Left Hand:** Traditionally considered unclean, use right hand for eating, greeting, giving/receiving items

## **Itinerary Customization Options**

### **For Families with Children:**
- Replace Museum of the Future with Dubai Aquarium or KidZania
- Add Dubai Dolphinarium or Children's City
- Consider Legoland Dubai instead of IMG Worlds
- Desert safari: Choose family-friendly options with earlier return

### **For Couples/Honeymooners:**
- Add romantic dinner at At.mosphere (Burj Khalifa, 122nd floor)
- Consider hot air balloon desert safari
- Private yacht cruise at sunset
- Couples spa day at luxury hotel

### **For Adventure Seekers:**
- Skydiving over Palm Jumeirah
- Zip lining at XLine Dubai Marina
- Deep sea fishing trip
- Indoor skiing at Ski Dubai (anytime)
- Desert quad biking or dune buggy riding

### **For Budget Travelers:**
- Stay in Deira or Bur Dubai areas
- Use public transport exclusively
- Eat at local cafeterias and street food stalls
- Free attractions: Dubai Fountain, JBR Beach, public parks, walking tours
- Visit during summer for lower hotel rates (but prepare for heat)

### **For Luxury Travelers:**
- Stay at Burj Al Arab, Armani Hotel, or Palazzo Versace
- Private guided tours of all attractions
- Helicopter tour of Dubai
- Luxury desert safari with private camp
- Fine dining at Michelin-starred restaurants
- Private yacht charter
