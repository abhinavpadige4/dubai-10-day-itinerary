DUBAI 10-DAY ITINERARY - DRAFT NOTES

DAY 1: ARRIVAL & DOWNTOWN EXPLORATION
- Arrival at Dubai International Airport (DXB)
- Transfer to hotel (Deira or Downtown area recommended for first-timers)
- Evening: Dubai Mall visit
  - Dubai Fountain show (free, every 30 minutes from 6 PM)
  - Dubai Aquarium & Underwater Zoo (optional: AED 149)
  - Dinner at Dubai Mall food court or specialty restaurants
- Transport: Metro Red Line to Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall station + taxi/walk
- Approximate Budget: AED 200-350 (excluding accommodation)

DAY 2: ICONIC LANDMARKS
- Morning: Burj Khalifa (At the Top SKY experience - pre-booked essential)
  - Ticket: AED 149-378 depending on time and level
  - Duration: 2-3 hours
- Late Morning: Dubai Mall exploration
  - Souk Al Bahar (traditional market adjacent to Dubai Mall)
  - Dubai Dino exhibit
- Lunch: At Dubai Mall (various options AED 50-150)
- Afternoon: Dubai Fountain lake walk
- Evening: Burj Khalifa light show viewing from Dubai Mall
- Dinner: At Burj Khalifa area or return to hotel
- Transport: Metro + walking/taxi
- Approximate Budget: AED 300-500

DAY 3: OLD DUBAI & CULTURAL HERITAGE
- Morning: Al Fahidi Historic District (Al Bastakiya)
  - Free entry, explore wind-tower architecture
  - Dubai Museum (Al Fahidi Fort): AED 3 adults, AED 1 children
- Late Morning: Abra ride across Dubai Creek
  - Traditional wooden boat: AED 1 per person
- Afternoon: Gold Souk & Spice Souk
  - Gold Souk: jewelry browsing (no pressure to buy)
  - Spice Souk: fragrant spices, nuts, dried fruits
- Lunch: Local Egyptian/Syrian cafeteria (try shawarma, falafel, hummus)
  - Approximate cost: AED 15-25 per person
- Evening: Dubai Creek dinner cruise (optional)
  - Dhow cruise with dinner: AED 150-200
- Transport: Metro to Al Fahidi + abra + walking/taxi
- Approximate Budget: AED 100-200

DAY 4: BEACH & MARINA LEISURE
- Morning: JBR Beach (Jumeirah Beach Residence)
  - Free public beach, water sports available (jet ski: AED 200-300/20 mins)
- Late Morning: The Walk at JBR
  - Outdoor shopping, dining, street performers
- Lunch: Beachfront restaurants at JBR (AED 80-200)
- Afternoon: Dubai Marina walk
  - Marina Mall visit
  - Yacht cruise booking (optional: AED 150-300/person for 2 hours)
- Evening: Dubai Marina sunset views
- Dinner: At Dubai Marina (many options AED 100-300)
- Transport: Metro to DMCC + tram/walk or taxi
- Approximate Budget: AED 250-450

DAY 5: DESERT SAFARI ADVENTURE
- Full Day: Desert Safari Experience
  - Pickup: 2:30-3:30 PM from hotel
  - Activities: Dune bashing (45 mins), camel riding, sandboarding
  - Bedouin-style camp: Arabic coffee, dates, henna painting
  - BBQ dinner with live entertainment (belly dancing, Tanoura show)
  - Return: 9:00-10:00 PM
  - Cost: AED 150-250 per person (includes transfers, activities, dinner)
- Morning: Free time or hotel pool relaxation
- Alternative Morning: Hot air balloon ride (AED 800-1000, sunrise)
- Transport: Included in safari package
- Approximate Budget: AED 200-300 (if not doing morning activity)

DAY 6: CULTURAL & MODERN DUBAI
- Morning: Museum of the Future
  - Ticket: AED 149 (pre-booking essential)
  - Duration: 2-3 hours
- Late Morning: Etihad Museum
  - Ticket: AED 25 adults, AED 10 students/children
  - Duration: 1-2 hours
- Lunch: City Walk area (AED 60-150)
- Afternoon: Jumeirah Mosque visit
  - Guided tour: AED 25 (includes refreshments, modest dress required)
  - Duration: 75 minutes
  - Timing: 10 AM & 2 PM daily (except Fridays)
- Evening: La Mer or Kite Beach relaxation
- Dinner: At La Mer or return to hotel area
- Transport: Metro/taxi between locations
- Approximate Budget: AED 250-400

DAY 7: ABU DHABI DAY TRIP
- Early Morning: Depart Dubai for Abu Dhabi (6:00-7:00 AM)
  - Distance: ~140 km, 1.5 hours each way
  - Transport options: Taxi (AED 250-300 round trip), 
                       Bus E100/A2 (AED 25 one way), 
                       Private transfer (AED 300-400)
- Morning: Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque
  - Free entry, guided tours available
  - Dress code: Modest clothing required (abaya/kandura provided if needed)
  - Duration: 1-1.5 hours
- Late Morning: Louvre Abu Dhabi
  - Ticket: AED 63 adults, AED 31.5 ages 13-22, free under 13
  - Duration: 2-3 hours
- Lunch: At Louvre Abu Dhabi café or Marina Mall area (AED 50-120)
- Afternoon: Qasr Al Watan (Presidential Palace)
  - Ticket: AED 60 adults, AED 30 children
  - Duration: 1-2 hours
- Alternative: Ferrari World or Yas Marina Circuit (if preferred)
- Evening: Return to Dubai
  - Optional: Stop at Global Village (seasonal: Oct-Apr, AED 15 entry)
- Dinner: Back in Dubai (AED 50-150)
- Transport: As arranged above
- Approximate Budget: AED 400-600 (including transport, entry fees, meals)

DAY 8: ADVENTURE & FAMILY FUN
- Option A: Theme Parks
  - IMG Worlds of Adventure: AED 299-399 (indoor theme park)
  - Dubai Parks and Resorts: AED 275-425 (Motiongate, Bollywood Parks, Legoland)
  - Duration: Full day
- Option B: Adventure Activities
  - Skydiving over Palm Jumeirah: AED 1,800-2,200
  - Hot air balloon desert flight: AED 800-1,000
  - Seaplane tour: AED 1,000-1,500
- Option C: Water Activities
  - Aquaventure Waterpark (Atlantis): AED 300-400
  - Wild Wadi Waterpark: AED 280-380
- Lunch: At theme park or activity venue (AED 80-180)
- Evening: Return to hotel, light dinner
- Transport: Taxi/private transfer to venues
- Approximate Budget: AED 350-800 (varies widely by activity choice)

DAY 9: RELAXATION & SHOPPING
- Morning: Kite Beach
  - Free beach access, volleyball, skatepark, food trucks
  - Morning swim or relaxation
- Late Morning: Brunch at Kite Beach cafes (AED 60-120)
- Afternoon: Mall of the Emirates
  - Shopping: International brands, local boutiques
  - Ski Dubai (optional): AED 230-350 for 2 hours slope access
  - Duration: 3-4 hours shopping
- Lunch: At Mall of the Emirates food court or restaurants (AED 50-150)
- Evening: City Walk
  - Outdoor shopping and dining district
  - Boulevard atmosphere, street art, fountains
- Dinner: At City Walk (many options AED 80-250)
- Transport: Metro to Mall of the Emirates + taxi/walk or direct taxi
- Approximate Budget: AED 250-450

DAY 10: FINAL DAY & DEPARTURE
- Morning: Last-minute souvenir shopping
  - Options: Gold Souk, Spice Souk, Dubai Mall, Ibn Battuta Mall
  - Budget: AED 200-500+ depending on purchases
- Alternative Morning: Dubai Frame
  - Ticket: AED 50 adults, AED 20 children
  - Duration: 1-1.5 hours
  - Panoramic views of old and new Dubai
- Lunch: Depending on flight time
  - Early flight: Light meal/snack at airport (AED 30-80)
  - Late flight: Proper meal in city (AED 50-150)
- Afternoon: Check-out from hotel
  - Luggage storage if needed (hotel usually provides)
- Transfer to Dubai International Airport (DXB)
  - Allow 3-4 hours before international flight
  - Taxi: AED 50-80 from city center
  - Metro: AED 5-15 (requires planning for luggage)
- Approximate Budget: AED 150-300 (excluding souvenirs/shopping)

PRACTICAL TIPS SECTION:
VISA REQUIREMENTS:
- GCC citizens: Visa-free entry
- Many nationalities: Visa on arrival (30-90 days, check eligibility)
- Others: Pre-arranged visa required
- Passport validity: Minimum 6 months from entry date

WEATHER & BEST TIME TO VISIT:
- Peak Season: November to March (pleasant weather: 20-30°C)
- Shoulder Season: April & October (warming up: 25-35°C)
- Low Season: May to September (very hot: 35-45°C+)
- Ramadan: Special considerations (reduced hours, no eating/drinking in public during daylight)

DRESS CODE:
- Public places: Modest clothing recommended (shoulders and knees covered)
- Beachwear: Acceptable at beaches and pools
- Mosques: Modest attire required (women: headscarf, long clothing; men: long pants)
- Evening venues: Smart casual to formal depending on venue

CURRENCY & PAYMENT:
- Currency: UAE Dirham (AED)
- Exchange rate: ~1 USD = 3.67 AED (fixed)
- Credit cards: Widely accepted (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- ATMs: Widely available
- Tipping: 10-15% in restaurants, small amounts for services

LANGUAGE:
- Official: Arabic
- Widely spoken: English (especially in tourism, business)
- Common: Hindi, Urdu, Tagalog, Persian

SIM CARD & CONNECTIVITY:
- Major providers: Etisalat, du
- Tourist SIMs: Available at airport (AED 50-100 for data packages)
- Free WiFi: Available in malls, hotels, many public areas
- International roaming: Check with home provider

TRANSPORT TIPS:
- Metro: Red and Green lines, efficient and affordable
  - NOL card required: AED 25 (includes AED 19 credit)
  - Daily cap: AED 20 (Red Line), AED 14 (Green Line)
- Taxis: Metered, start at AED 5, AED 1.82/km
  - Careem/Uber: Competitive pricing
- Buses: Extensive network, AED 3-7 per journey
- Water Transport: Abras (AED 1), water taxis, ferries
- Car Rental: Available but traffic can be challenging

CULTURAL ETIQUETTE:
- During Ramadan: No eating, drinking, or smoking in public during daylight hours
- Public displays of affection: Keep to minimum
- Photography: Ask permission before photographing people
- Friday: Holy day, some businesses have altered hours
- Left hand: Traditionally considered unclean, use right hand for eating/giving

HEALTH & SAFETY:
- Tap water: Safe to drink but bottled water preferred for taste
- Healthcare: Excellent private and public facilities
- Insurance: Travel insurance strongly recommended
- Emergency: 999 (police, ambulance, fire)
- Pharmacies: Widely available, many open 24/7

USEFUL APPS:
- RTA Dubai: Public transport planner
- Careem/Uber: Ride-hailing
- Entertainer: Buy-one-get-one-free dining/attractions
- Zomato/Tripadvisor: Restaurant reviews
- Dubai Calendar: Events and activities
