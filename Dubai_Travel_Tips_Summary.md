# Dubai Travel Tips Summary

## Quick Reference Guide

### Essential Information
- **Currency**: UAE Dirham (AED) - 1 USD ≈ 3.67 AED
- **Language**: Arabic (official), English widely spoken
- **Time Zone**: GST (UTC+4)
- **Electricity**: 220-240V, Type G (UK-style) plugs
- **Weekend**: Friday-Saturday (many businesses closed Friday morning)

### Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season**: November-March (20-30°C / 68-86°F)
- **Shoulder**: April & October (25-35°C / 77-95°F)
- **Low Season**: May-September (35-45°C+ / 95-113°F+)
- **Ramadan**: Varies yearly - check dates

### Transportation
- **Metro**: Red & Green lines, NOL card required (AED 25 for tourist card)
- **Taxis**: Flag fall AED 5, plus AED 1.82/km
- **Ride-hailing**: Careem & Uber widely available
- **Buses**: AED 3-7 per journey, NOL card required
- **Water**: Abras AED 1 (creek crossing)

### Key Attractions & Costs
- **Burj Khalifa (At the Top)**: AED 149-378
- **Dubai Museum**: AED 3
- **Desert Safari**: AED 150-250 (includes dinner)
- **Theme Parks**: AED 275-400+
- **Museum of the Future**: AED 149
- **Louvre Abu Dhabi**: AED 63
- **Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque**: FREE
- **Dubai Fountain Show**: FREE

### Food Recommendations
- **Breakfast**: AED 25-60 (hotel, café, street food)
- **Lunch**: AED 30-150 (food court, casual, fine dining)
- **Dinner**: AED 50-250+ (casual to fine dining)
- **Must-try**: Shawarma, falafel, hummus, fresh juices, dates, Arabic coffee

### Cultural Etiquette
- **Dress**: Modest in public (shoulders/knees covered), beachwear at beaches/pools only
- **Ramadan**: No eating/drinking in public during daylight hours
- **Photography**: Ask permission before photographing people
- **Friday**: Holy day - some businesses open later
- **Public Affection**: Keep to minimum (holding hands generally OK)

### Practical Tips
- **Water**: Bottled recommended for taste (tap water safe)
- **Sunscreen**: SPF 30+ essential year-round
- **Hydration**: Drink plenty of water, especially outdoors
- **NOL Card**: Get for metro/bus/tram (AED 25 tourist card)
- **Bargaining**: Acceptable in souks, not in malls/fixed price stores
- **Emergency**: 999 (police, ambulance, fire)
- **Pharmacies**: Many open 24/7 in major areas
- **WiFi**: Free in malls, hotels, many cafes

### Useful Apps
- **Transport**: RTA Dubai, Careem, Uber
- **Food**: Zomato, Talabat, Deliveroo, Entertainer (BOGOF)
- **Navigation**: Google Maps, Waze
- **Events**: Dubai Calendar, Visit Dubai
- **Government**: Dubai Now (services, fines, etc.)

### Packing Suggestions
- **Clothing**: Light, modest clothing + swimwear + light jacket for evenings/desert
- **Footwear**: Comfortable walking shoes + sandals + closed-toe for desert activities
- **Essentials**: Passport, visa (if required), travel insurance, medications
- **Tech**: Phone, charger, adapter (Type G), camera, power bank
- **Extras**: Sunglasses, hat, reusable water bottle, small day bag

### Health & Safety
- **Healthcare**: Excellent facilities, travel insurance strongly recommended
- **Safety**: Very low crime rate, standard precautions advised
- **Emergency**: 999 for all emergencies
- **Pharmacies**: Look for green cross sign, many 24/7
- **Heat**: Dehydration risk in summer - drink water, avoid midday sun
- **COVID**: Check latest requirements before travel

### Shopping Tips
- **Souks**: Gold, Spice, Textile - bargaining expected
- **Malls**: Tax-free shopping available for tourists
- **Hours**: Typically 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM (malls), varied for souks
- **Fragrances**: Arabic perfumes (oud, bakhoor) popular gifts
- **Dates**: Premium dates make excellent gifts
- **Gold**: Competitive prices, verify purity and make charges

### Communication
- **SIM Cards**: Etisalat or du tourist packs (AED 50-100 for data)
- **WiFi**: Widely available in hotels, malls, cafes
- **Roaming**: Check with provider - often expensive vs local SIM
- **Country Code**: +971
- **Emergency**: 999 works from any phone

### Departure
- **Airport**: Allow 3-4 hours for international flights
- **Transport**: Taxi AED 50-80 from city, Metro AED 5-15
- **Luggage**: Most hotels offer storage post-checkout
- **Duty Free**: Check allowances for your destination country
- **Final Bills**: Settle all hotel charges before departure
