# Dubai Travel Practical Tips Summary

## Quick Reference Guide

### **Essential Info at a Glance**
- **Currency:** UAE Dirham (AED) | 1 USD ≈ 3.67 AED
- **Best Time:** November-March (20-28°C / 68-82°F)
- **Language:** Arabic (official), English widely spoken
- **Visa:** Most nationalities get visa on arrival (check requirements)
- **Time Zone:** GST (UTC+4)
- **Emergency:** 999 (police/ambulance/fire)

### **Daily Budget Ranges (Per Person)**
- **Budget:** AED 250-350/day ($68-95)
- **Mid-Range:** AED 600-800/day ($163-218) 
- **Comfort:** AED 1,000-1,500/day ($272-408)
- **Luxury:** AED 2,000+/day ($544+)

### **Must-Try Foods & Average Costs**
- **Shawarma wrap:** AED 8-15
- **Falafel sandwich:** AED 5-10
- **Hummus & pita:** AED 10-15
- **Machboos (spiced rice):** AED 25-40
- **Luqaimat (sweet dumplings):** AED 10-15
- **Arabic coffee:** Often free in hotels/malls
- **Fresh juice:** AED 8-12
- **Mandi (Yemeni rice dish):** AED 20-35

### **Transport Costs (Per Trip)**
- **Metro:** AED 3-7.50 (based on zones)
- **Bus:** AED 3-5
- **Tram:** AED 3
- **Taxi (short):** AED 12-20
- **Taxi (Dubai Mall to Burj Khalifa):** AED 8-12
- **Taxi (Airport to Downtown):** AED 50-70
- **Nol Card:** AED 25 (card) + load balance

### **Attraction Ticket Prices**
- **Burj Khalifa (At the Top):** AED 149-378
- **Dubai Aquarium:** AED 120
- **Desert Safari (shared):** AED 200-250
- **Museum of the Future:** AED 149
- **Louvre Abu Dhabi:** AED 63
- **Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque:** Free
- **Qasr Al Watan:** AED 65
- **Dubai Frame:** AED 50
- **IMG Worlds:** AED 295-345
- **Ski Dubai:** AED 190-250

### **Cultural Etiquette Quick Tips**
✅ **DO:**
- Dress modestly in public (shoulders/knees covered)
- Remove shoes when entering homes/mosques
- Use right hand for eating/giving/receiving
- Accept Arabic coffee when offered (it's polite)
- Ask before photographing people
- Haggle in souks (start at 50% asking price)

❌ **DON'T:**
- Eat/drink/smoke in public during Ramadan daylight hours
- Show public affection beyond holding hands
- Drink and drive (zero tolerance policy)
- Photograph government buildings/military
- Point feet at people or religious objects
- Use left hand for eating/giving items

### **Shopping Guide**
**Tax-Free:** Available for tourists (min. AED 250 purchase)
**Best Areas:**
- **Luxury:** Dubai Mall, Mall of Emirates
- **Traditional:** Gold Souk, Spice Souk, Textile Souk
- **Outlet:** Dubai Outlet Mall, Ibn Battuta Mall
- **Local:** City Walk, Boxpark, Mercato

**Bargaining:** Expected in souks, not in malls
**Hours:** Malls 10 AM-10 PM, Souks 9 AM-1PM & 4-9PM

### **Communication**
**SIM Cards:** Etisalat/du at airport (AED 50-100 for tourist pack)
**Wi-Fi:** Free in malls, hotels, cafes, public areas
**Country Code:** +971
**Useful Apps:** RTA Dubai, Careem/Uber, Entertainer, Zomato, Visit Dubai

### **Health & Safety**
**Safety:** One of world's safest cities (low crime)
**Water:** Bottled/recommended (tap safe but many prefer bottled)
**Pharmacies:** Green cross signs, 24-hour in major areas
**Sun Protection:** Essential year-round (very high UV index)
**Insurance:** Strongly recommended for medical coverage

### **Day Trip to Abu Dhabi Essentials**
**Duration:** ~1.5 hours each way
**Key Sites:**
- **Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque:** Free, modest dress required
- **Louvre Abu Dhabi:** AED 63 (book online)
- **Qasr Al Watan:** AED 65 (Presidential Palace)
- **Emirates Palace:** View exterior, afternoon tea AED 150+
- **Corniche Beach:** Free public beach
- **Ferrari World:** AED 295-345 (if interested)

**Transport Options:**
- **Organized Tour:** AED 250-350 (includes transport/guide)
- **Taxi:** AED 200-250 each way
- **Rental Car:** AED 150/day + fuel
- **Public Bus:** E100/A101 (~AED 25 each way, 2+ hours)

### **Seasonal Packing List**
**Nov-Mar (Peak Season):**
- Light layers + sweater/jacket
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Scarf/pashmina (versatile for modesty)
- Sunglasses, sunscreen (SPF 50+)
- Swimsuit (hotel pools often heated)

**Apr-Oct (Hot Season):**
- Light, breathable fabrics (cotton, linen)
- Wide-brimmed hat or cap
- High SPF sunscreen + lip balm
- Reusable water bottle (essential)
- Sandals + closed shoes for malls
- Light scarf for air-conditioned places

### **Money-Saving Strategies**
1. **Nol Card** for public transport (vs. individual tickets)
2. **Lunch specials** at fine dining (often 50% dinner price)
3. **Entertainer app** for 2-for-1 deals
4. **Free attractions:** Dubai Fountain, JBR Beach, mosque exteriors
5. **Combine tickets** (Burj Khalifa + Aquarium, etc.)
6. **Stay in Deira/Bur Dubai** for better hotel rates
7. **Eat where locals eat** (Indian/Pakistani/Lebanese spots)
8. **Shop at outlet malls** and during sales seasons
9. **Use hotel amenities** (pool, beach, gym if available)
10. **Travel shoulder season** (Apr-May, Sep-Oct) for balance

### **Emergency Contacts**
- **Police/Ambulance/Fire:** 999
- **Tourist Police:** 901 (English-speaking)
- **Dubai Tourism:** 800 4444
- **Hotel:** Keep front desk number handy
- **Embassy:** Your country's embassy contact info

### **Quick Day Planning**
**Morning (8 AM-12 PM):** Best for outdoor activities, sightseeing
**Afternoon (12-4 PM):** Indoor attractions, shopping, rest (hot in summer)
**Evening (4-9 PM):** Prime time for most activities, dining
**Night (9 PM+):** Entertainment, night views, late dining

**Ramadan Adjustments:** Many restaurants closed day, open after sunset (Iftar)
**Friday:** Weekly holy day - some attractions open afternoon, busy evenings
