## 📋 Overview

This repository contains a comprehensive **10-day Dubai itinerary** designed for first-time visitors seeking the perfect blend of modern marvels, cultural experiences, desert adventures, and relaxation. The itinerary covers iconic landmarks, authentic Emirati culture, world-class shopping, and a memorable day trip to Abu Dhabi.

## 🗺️ What's Included

- **Day-by-day schedule** for 10 full days in Dubai
- **Detailed attractions** including Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, Palm Jumeirah, and more
- **Cultural experiences** such as Al Fahidi Historic District, Dubai Museum, and traditional souks
- **Food recommendations** with approximate costs for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks
- **Transportation tips** covering Metro, taxi, bus, and rideshare options
- **Budget breakdown** including accommodation, food, transport, and attractions
- **Practical tips** covering visa, weather, dress code, currency, and more
- **One day trip to Abu Dhabi** featuring Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Louvre Abu Dhabi, and Qasr Al Watan

## 💰 Budget Estimates (Per Person)

- **Budget Traveler**: AED 3,500-5,000 (USD 950-1,360)
- **Mid-Range Traveler**: AED 6,000-8,500 (USD 1,630-2,315)
- **Comfortable Traveler**: AED 9,000-13,000+ (USD 2,450-3,540+)

*Note: Estimates exclude international flights and personal shopping.*
